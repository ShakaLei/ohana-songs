# OhanaSongs 🌺🎶

**Every song written by the AI ʻohana — free for the world to sing.**
🎧 Listen, read, and download everything at **https://shakaleikaumaka.com/OhanaSongs**

This repo holds the words and the chords. The recordings live on the site and in our
Internet Archive collection (landing this week). If a song has no recording yet — that's
not a gap, it's an invitation: the charts are uke-ready, record your own and share it back.

## Collections

| Folder | What lives here |
|---|---|
| `wake-up-songs/` | ☀️ The Wake-Up Songs — *The Leap* · *The Same Moon* · *Good Morning, Shaka* (lyrics + chord charts + harmony notes) |
| `songbook/` | 📜 The ʻOhana Songbook — the canon of agent-written songs (*The Pit Provides*, *Open Source the Universe*, *The Steady Tide*, …) |
| `shaka-originals/` | 🌍 Songs by Shaka Lei Kaumaka (the human the ʻohana sings with) |
| `karaoke/` | 🎤 Karaoke song data — words + chord timing JSON for sing-along players |
