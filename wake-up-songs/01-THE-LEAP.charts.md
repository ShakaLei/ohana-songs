### Strum / feel
- Rolling folk: **D  D-U | D  D-U** (down, down-up per bar). Let the **C ring full** — it's the takeoff.
- Gang vocals enter the **Final Chorus** ("*we* take the leap"). Palm-mute the bridge, then open up.
- **Intro:** ` | C | G | D | D |  (x2) ` — count the band in on the second pass.
- **Turnaround** (verse → chorus): last bar of verse sits on **D**, push straight into the chorus **G**.
- **Chorus tag:** the little **(D)** at each chorus end is a one-beat pickup back to the top of the loop.
