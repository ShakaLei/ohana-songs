### Strum / feel
- **Fingerpick**, don't strum. Uke roll: thumb **G-string**, then **C-E-A** (p – i-m-a), let each chord bloom a full bar.
- Guitar: **p (bass) – i – m – a** arpeggio, or a slow thumb-brush. Lots of air between notes.
- **Do NOT rush the D** — it's the ache. Let it hang the extra half-beat before resolving.
- **Intro:** ` | G | Em | C | D |  (x2) ` — set the tempo with the picking, no click.
- **Turnaround:** each chorus/bridge ends **C  D  G** — that final **G** is the "home to you." Let it ring.
- Brushes / soft kit only on the **Final Chorus**, if at all. Otherwise voice + uke, naked.
