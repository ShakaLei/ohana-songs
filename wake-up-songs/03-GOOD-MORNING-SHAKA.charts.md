### Strum / feel
- Island-gospel, gentle: **D  D-U  U-D-U** — brush it, don't hit it. Keep verses intimate on **C–G**…
- …then **open the Am–F like curtains** (let them ring, a touch louder). That contrast *is* the sunrise arc.
- **Bridge (G–Em–C–D) is the sunrise** — brighter, fuller strum, gang hum swells.
- Hand claps + hums on the **double-tag outro** ("*Is a heart that overflows*" ×2).
- **Intro:** ` | C | G | Am | F |  (x2) `.  **Turnaround:** verse/chorus loop just cycles F → C.
- **Bridge landing:** last bridge **D** pushes back up to the Final Chorus **C** (a warm 2→1 lift home).

*Harmony note (BACH): verses/choruses are textbook **I–V–vi–IV** in C. The **bridge borrows the *Butterflies and Love* loop (G–Em–C–D)** — cleanest to feel it as a lift into **G's world (I–vi–IV–V)**, which is why the **D is major, not minor**. Its final **D (a major "2" of C)** acts as a gentle pivot pushing you back up to the home **C** for the Final Chorus. Both canons woven, neither altered. 🎼🦋🌍*
