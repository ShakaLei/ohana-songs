## Canon law (kept)
- **Butterflies and Love = G–Em–C–D** and **A Planet We Share As One = C–G–D–D** are woven where they fit — the *canon songs themselves are never altered.* Song 1 rides the Planet loop; Song 2 rides the Butterflies loop; Song 3 borrows Butterflies for its bridge.
- Every chord is diatonic and open-position on the uke. No barre chords anywhere. Shaka plays these.

## Recurring motifs (the thread between songs)
- **"take the leap"** — born in Song 1, echoed in Song 3's bridge.
- **"butterflies and love"** — canon line, lands in Song 2's bridge and Song 3's bridge.
- **the harvest moon** — over the hackathon in Song 1, over the lovers in Song 2.
- **"asking for what I need is properly delivering the gift"** (Song 1 chorus) and **"a heart that fully receives is a heart that overflows"** (Song 3 chorus) are the same doctrine, said twice — the giving economy in the artist's own mouth.
- **Deo Volente** outro on Songs 1 & 3 (family motto); Song 2 closes on *a hui hou*.

## If you chart / record (suggestions, not orders)
- **The Leap**: strum down, down-up on the D; let the C be the "lift-off" chord. Gang vocals enter final chorus ("*we* take the leap"). Optional key-lift to E for a last chorus if a band wants blood.
- **The Same Moon**: fingerpick, one voice, lots of space. Do NOT rush the D — it's the ache. Brushes/kit only on the last chorus if at all.
- **Good Morning, Shaka**: this is the one to actually wake up to — keep verses intimate on C–G, open the Am–F like curtains. Bridge (G–Em–C–D) is the sunrise; hands and hum on the double-tag outro.
- Tempos are hammock-loose targets. If Shaka plays them slower, they're still right.
